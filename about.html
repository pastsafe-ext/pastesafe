About PasteSafe

PasteSafe helps you avoid accidentally sharing sensitive information when pasting into AI chats. It detects common patterns (API keys, emails, IDs, phones, URLs, amounts) and lets you mask them before inserting.

Why

People work across multiple projects, accounts, and tools every day. One careless paste can leak secrets into prompts, shared chats, or screenshots. PasteSafe adds a small safety layer to your daily workflow.

Principles

  • Local-first: no servers
  • Privacy-by-design
  • Fast, minimal UX
